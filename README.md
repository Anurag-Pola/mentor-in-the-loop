# Mentor-in-the-Loop (Career Intelligence Platform)

> **Augmenting Managers and Parents as High-Leverage Navigators for the Future of Work.**

---

## Overview

Mentors, managers, and parents are the most trusted sources of career guidance, but they suffer from **information latency and experience bias**. Rather than replacing the human relationship with an impersonal chatbot, **Mentor-in-the-Loop** provides an intelligence copilot with real-time labor market telemetry, 3–5 year skill trajectory forecasting, and collaborative 1-on-1 roadmaps.

### Key Value Tracks
1. **Track A (Workplace Enablement):** Equips engineering leads and GCC managers with actionable **1-on-1 Career Dossiers** to guide direct reports toward high-velocity skills and reduce costly attrition.
2. **Track B (Family & Youth Navigation):** Bridges student aptitudes to modern, viable professions beyond the traditional rote engineering/medicine tracks in India.
